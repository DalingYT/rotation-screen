# Rotation Screen

A Geode mod that adds a chaotic visual challenge by randomly rotating your screen during gameplay.

---

### Features

- **Random Rotations**: Rotates your view to 0°, 90°, 180°, or 270° at randomized intervals.
- **Pause Safe**: Automatically postpones rotation triggers while the game is paused to prevent camera glitches and desync.
- **Auto Reset**: Instantly resets the view back to 0° when dying or restarting the level.

---

### Configuration

You can customize the mod's behavior directly from the **Geode** settings menu:

- **Min Interval**: Minimum wait time (in seconds) before the screen rotates.
- **Max Interval**: Maximum wait time (in seconds) before the screen rotates.
- **Rotation Speed**: Duration (in seconds) of the rotation animation.
